Initial implementation (2026-10-19): REST v2 datasets, tables, tabledata, jobs and jobs.query verified against cloud.google.com/go/bigquery v1.85.0 through the official client in the package tests (dataset and table lifecycle, streaming inserts with de-duplication, parameterised queries, nested and repeated round trips, load/extract/copy including Parquet, DML and DDL, paging). The GCS hooks are exercised against the real GCS emulator in internal/app. Wire shapes were read from google.golang.org/api/bigquery/v2 and the client's value decoder (TIMESTAMP is integer microseconds under useInt64Timestamp); behaviour of the live service was not available for comparison.
