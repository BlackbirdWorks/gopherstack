## 2026-11-13 pass

- Shapes follow https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/mysql-database/doc/supported-shapes.html: MySQL.Free is 1 ECPU and 8 GiB, MySQL.96 and MySQL.192 exist, MySQL.256 has 1024 GiB, and the E3, E4, Standard2, Standard3 and Optimized3 OCPU families are listed. The `.HA` suffixed names of the page are not listed.
- Retention: automatic backups 1 to 35 days (default 7), manual backups 1 to 365 days; a soft-delete backup past its retention stays DELETE_SCHEDULED for 7 days, can be deleted at once or restored with CancelBackupDeletion, and cannot be moved, copied or restored meanwhile (https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/mysql-database/doc/overview-backups.html). CancelBackupDeletion is POST /backups/{backupId}/actions/cancelDeletion (mysql/mysql_dbbackups_client.go) and answers 202 with a work request (UPDATE_BACKUP).
- Reserved administrator names match https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/mysql-database/doc/reserved-usernames.html.
- CreateDbSystem, CreateBackup, CreateChannel, CreateConfiguration, AddHeatWaveCluster, Start/Stop/RestartDbSystem, Start/Stop/RestartHeatWaveCluster, UpdateChannel, ResumeChannel and ResetChannel replay on opc-retry-token for 24 hours (SDK request docs) and answer 409 InvalidatedRetryToken once the resource was deleted (https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/API/References/apierrors.htm); tokens persist in the snapshot as retryTokens.
